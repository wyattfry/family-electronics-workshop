# Resources

These are the existing books, kits and sites we borrow from. None of them covers this whole
course, but several are worth owning.

## Books

| Book | Use it for | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Charles Platt, *Make: Electronics* (3rd ed.) | The best sequenced, hands-on book. Its numbered experiments make great "side quests" between our units. | 1–9 |
| Charles Platt, *Make: More Electronics* | Its sequel: logic, op-amps, sensors. | 5–10 |
| Øyvind Nydal Dahl, *Electronics for Kids* (No Starch) | Kid-level explanations. Good reading for the 8 y.o. | 1–5 |
| Forrest Mims, *Getting Started in Electronics* | Classic hand-drawn explanations of how parts work. | 1–5 |
| Forrest Mims, *Engineer's Mini-Notebook* series (555, op-amp, sensor projects) | Circuit cookbooks. The Punk Console comes from here. | 4–5 |
| H. Ward Silver, *Hands-On Radio Experiments* (ARRL) | A sequenced set of radio experiments. | 2, 13, 15 |
| ARRL, *Ham Radio License Manual* (Technician) | License study. | 13 |
| Wes Hayward W7ZOI et al., *Experimental Methods in RF Design* | Manhattan-style RF homebrew. For the parent. | 15 |

## Free, online

- ***Soldering Is Easy*** comic (Mitch Altman, Andie Nordgren, Jeff Keyzer). Read it
  before Unit 1.
- **Adafruit Learning System** (learn.adafruit.com): guides for the MAX98357A, the Pico and servos.
- **SparkFun tutorials**: "How to Solder: Through-Hole", "Voltage Dividers", "Pull-up Resistors".
- **buildelectroniccircuits.com**: Øyvind Dahl's site, with kid-friendly circuits.
- **HamStudy.org**: license exam practice with spaced repetition. Free.
- **homingin.com**: Joe Moell K0OV's radio direction-finding and fox-hunting site
  (offset attenuator, antennas, event ideas).
- **WB2HOL tape-measure Yagi**: the classic fox-hunt antenna design. Search "WB2HOL tape
  measure beam".
- **Phil Schatzmann's ESP32-A2DP** (github.com/pschatzmann/ESP32-A2DP): the Bluetooth
  audio library used in Unit 11.
- **Scoppy** (Android app plus Pico firmware): turns a Raspberry Pi Pico into a
  2-channel scope. Used in Unit 8.
- **KiCad** (kicad.org): free PCB design software.

## Suppliers

- **Parts, US:** DigiKey, Mouser, Tayda (cheap basics), Jameco
- **Speakers:** Parts Express (Dayton Audio drivers, passive radiators, enclosure kits)
- **Modules:** Adafruit, SparkFun, Pololu (motors and robot parts)
- **PCB fabs**, for when a hand-etched board won't do: JLCPCB, PCBWay, OSH Park

## Community

- A **local ham radio club**. Many run kit-build nights, fox hunts and youth programs, and
  hold license exam sessions. Probably the best "curriculum" for Units 12, 13 and 15.
- **ARRL Kids Day** (January and June): a good first on-air event.
- A local **makerspace**, if you want access to a laser cutter or CNC for enclosures.
